Housing and rent in British Columbia

Housing is the top concern in two recent BC polls. Here is what voters say, what the government reports, and what each party has published, with a source for every claim.

What voters say

  • Research Co. (Aug 12–14, 2026): 29% name housing, poverty and homelessness as the most important issue facing BC, the top issue overall; among ages 35–54 it is 36% (Research Co.).
  • Leger (Apr 3–6, 2026): 30% name housing affordability as a top concern, alongside healthcare at 29%; government approval on housing, healthcare and the economy is below 50% (Leger).

The facts

The BC government reports the following in a June 2, 2026 news release (news.gov.bc.ca). These are government claims, not independent findings:

  • Average asking rent in BC fell 12.5%, from $2,671 in August 2023 to $2,338 in April 2026, a reduction of $333 a month.
  • Average asking rent in Vancouver and Burnaby is more than $650 lower than the 2023 peak, and BC has the largest year-over-year asking-rent declines of any province.
  • BC Builds broke ground on its 4,000th unit, with nearly 820 new homes in Burnaby.

How to read this: asking rent is what landlords advertise for available units. It does not describe what existing tenants pay, and it says nothing about home prices or ownership.

What to watch

Watch for any party publishing costed housing measures, and independent rent and starts data that can be compared with the government's asking-rent figures.

FAQ

Have BC rents gone down?

The BC government says average asking rent fell 12.5% between August 2023 ($2,671) and April 2026 ($2,338) (news release). Asking rents are advertised prices for available units, not what existing tenants pay.

What do voters say is BC's biggest issue?

In Research Co.'s August 2026 poll, 29% said housing, poverty and homelessness, ahead of the economy and jobs (24%) and health care (21%) (Research Co.).

What is BC Builds?

The government describes BC Builds as its program to build homes; it says the program broke ground on its 4,000th unit in June 2026 (release).
