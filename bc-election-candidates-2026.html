Candidates

BC election candidates 2026

Nominations closed October 3 at 1 p.m. Pacific time. Elections BC now publishes the final candidate list for the October 24 general election. See the official candidate list.

Where things stand

Under Elections BC's process, a nomination is official once Elections BC accepts it; see candidate nominations and the current candidate list. Elections BC states that the October 3 list is final. Check the live official record for each riding and candidate affiliation.

The official list is the authoritative source for ballot candidates and affiliations. The Abbotsford-Mission by-election, which had five confirmed candidates, was cancelled September 22 when the general election was called; see the Abbotsford-Mission page.

Who is leading the parties

The party leaders are the people voters will see across the campaign. See BC party leaders 2026. For polling see BC election polls, and for key dates see the BC election 2026 guide.

There are 93 electoral districts in BC; each elects one MLA. Riding information, including the 2024 result for each seat, is on the BC ridings page.
