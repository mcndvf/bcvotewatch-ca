Prince George-Mackenzie

Kiel Giddens (Conservative) won Prince George-Mackenzie in the October 19, 2024 BC election with 60.92% of the vote, 6,065 votes ahead of Shar McCrory (NDP). Turnout was 59.50%.

How close was it?

Prince George-Mackenzie ranks 83 of 93 among the closest races in 2024 (1 is the closest). The winning margin was 6,065 votes (32.68 percentage points); it would have taken 3,033 voters switching from Kiel Giddens to Shar McCrory to flip the result. Province-wide the Conservative took 43.28% of the vote, compared with 60.92% here.

Prince George-Mackenzie FAQ

Who won Prince George-Mackenzie in the 2024 BC election?

Kiel Giddens (Conservative) won Prince George-Mackenzie with 11,307 votes (60.92%), ahead of Shar McCrory (NDP) with 5,242 (28.24%). Source: Elections BC Statement of Votes.

What was voter turnout in Prince George-Mackenzie in 2024?

18,588 of 31,239 registered voters voted in Prince George-Mackenzie, a turnout of 59.50%, compared with 58.45% province-wide.

How close was the race in Prince George-Mackenzie?
