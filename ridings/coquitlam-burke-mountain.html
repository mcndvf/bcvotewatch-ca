Coquitlam-Burke Mountain

Jodie Wickens (NDP) won Coquitlam-Burke Mountain in the October 19, 2024 BC election with 50.85% of the vote, 368 votes ahead of Stephen Frolek (Conservative). Turnout was 54.69%.

How close was it?

Coquitlam-Burke Mountain ranks 9 of 93 among the closest races in 2024 (1 is the closest). The winning margin was 368 votes (1.70 percentage points); it would have taken 185 voters switching from Jodie Wickens to Stephen Frolek to flip the result. Province-wide the NDP took 44.87% of the vote, compared with 50.85% here.

Coquitlam-Burke Mountain FAQ

Who won Coquitlam-Burke Mountain in the 2024 BC election?

Jodie Wickens (NDP) won Coquitlam-Burke Mountain with 11,020 votes (50.85%), ahead of Stephen Frolek (Conservative) with 10,652 (49.15%). Source: Elections BC Statement of Votes.

What was voter turnout in Coquitlam-Burke Mountain in 2024?

21,766 of 39,801 registered voters voted in Coquitlam-Burke Mountain, a turnout of 54.69%, compared with 58.45% province-wide.

How close was the race in Coquitlam-Burke Mountain?

The winning margin was 368 votes (1.70 percentage points), the 9th closest of BC's 93 ridings.
