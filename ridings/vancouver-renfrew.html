Vancouver-Renfrew

Adrian Dix (NDP) won Vancouver-Renfrew in the October 19, 2024 BC election with 63.22% of the vote, 5,656 votes ahead of Tom Ikonomou (Conservative). Turnout was 48.24%.

How close was it?

Vancouver-Renfrew ranks 82 of 93 among the closest races in 2024 (1 is the closest). The winning margin was 5,656 votes (32.56 percentage points); it would have taken 2,829 voters switching from Adrian Dix to Tom Ikonomou to flip the result. Province-wide the NDP took 44.87% of the vote, compared with 63.22% here.

Vancouver-Renfrew FAQ

Who won Vancouver-Renfrew in the 2024 BC election?

Adrian Dix (NDP) won Vancouver-Renfrew with 10,983 votes (63.22%), ahead of Tom Ikonomou (Conservative) with 5,327 (30.66%). Source: Elections BC Statement of Votes.

What was voter turnout in Vancouver-Renfrew in 2024?

17,421 of 36,116 registered voters voted in Vancouver-Renfrew, a turnout of 48.24%, compared with 58.45% province-wide.

How close was the race in Vancouver-Renfrew?

The winning margin was 5,656 votes (32.56 percentage points), the 82nd closest of BC's 93 ridings.
