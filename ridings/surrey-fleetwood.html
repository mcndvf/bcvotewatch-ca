Surrey-Fleetwood

Jagrup Brar (NDP) won Surrey-Fleetwood in the October 19, 2024 BC election with 48.61% of the vote, 753 votes ahead of Avtar Gill (Conservative). Turnout was 54.14%.

How close was it?

Surrey-Fleetwood ranks 19 of 93 among the closest races in 2024 (1 is the closest). The winning margin was 753 votes (3.69 percentage points); it would have taken 377 voters switching from Jagrup Brar to Avtar Gill to flip the result. Province-wide the NDP took 44.87% of the vote, compared with 48.61% here.
