Vancouver-Quilchena

Dallas Brodie (Conservative) won Vancouver-Quilchena in the October 19, 2024 BC election with 51.58% of the vote, 2,815 votes ahead of Callista Ryan (NDP). Turnout was 58.45%.

How close was it?

Vancouver-Quilchena ranks 46 of 93 among the closest races in 2024 (1 is the closest). The winning margin was 2,815 votes (12.67 percentage points); it would have taken 1,408 voters switching from Dallas Brodie to Callista Ryan to flip the result. Province-wide the Conservative took 43.28% of the vote, compared with 51.58% here.
