Burnaby Centre

Anne Kang (NDP) won Burnaby Centre in the October 19, 2024 BC election with 57.28% of the vote, 2,486 votes ahead of Dharam Kajal (Conservative). Turnout was 50.11%.

How close was it?

Burnaby Centre ranks 52 of 93 among the closest races in 2024 (1 is the closest). The winning margin was 2,486 votes (14.56 percentage points); it would have taken 1,244 voters switching from Anne Kang to Dharam Kajal to flip the result. Province-wide the NDP took 44.87% of the vote, compared with 57.28% here.

Burnaby Centre FAQ

Who won Burnaby Centre in the 2024 BC election?

Anne Kang (NDP) won Burnaby Centre with 9,780 votes (57.28%), ahead of Dharam Kajal (Conservative) with 7,294 (42.72%). Source: Elections BC Statement of Votes.

What was voter turnout in Burnaby Centre in 2024?

17,182 of 34,290 registered voters voted in Burnaby Centre, a turnout of 50.11%, compared with 58.45% province-wide.

How close was the race in Burnaby Centre?

The winning margin was 2,486 votes (14.56 percentage points), the 52nd closest of BC's 93 ridings.
