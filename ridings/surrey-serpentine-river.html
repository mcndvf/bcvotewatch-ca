Surrey-Serpentine River

Linda Hepner (Conservative) won Surrey-Serpentine River in the October 19, 2024 BC election with 49.70% of the vote, 435 votes ahead of Baltej Singh Dhillon (NDP). Turnout was 54.49%.

How close was it?

Surrey-Serpentine River ranks 13 of 93 among the closest races in 2024 (1 is the closest). The winning margin was 435 votes (2.21 percentage points); it would have taken 218 voters switching from Linda Hepner to Baltej Singh Dhillon to flip the result. Province-wide the Conservative took 43.28% of the vote, compared with 49.70% here.

Who won Surrey-Serpentine River in the 2024 BC election?

Linda Hepner (Conservative) won Surrey-Serpentine River with 9,782 votes (49.70%), ahead of Baltej Singh Dhillon (NDP) with 9,347 (47.49%). Source: Elections BC Statement of Votes.

What was voter turnout in Surrey-Serpentine River in 2024?

19,722 of 36,196 registered voters voted in Surrey-Serpentine River, a turnout of 54.49%, compared with 58.45% province-wide.
