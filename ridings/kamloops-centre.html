Kamloops Centre

Peter Milobar (Conservative) won Kamloops Centre in the October 19, 2024 BC election with 48.83% of the vote, 2,003 votes ahead of Kamal Grewal (NDP). Turnout was 57.59%.

How close was it?

Kamloops Centre ranks 31 of 93 among the closest races in 2024 (1 is the closest). The winning margin was 2,003 votes (7.91 percentage points); it would have taken 1,002 voters switching from Peter Milobar to Kamal Grewal to flip the result. Province-wide the Conservative took 43.28% of the vote, compared with 48.83% here.

Kamloops Centre FAQ

Who won Kamloops Centre in the 2024 BC election?

Peter Milobar (Conservative) won Kamloops Centre with 12,372 votes (48.83%), ahead of Kamal Grewal (NDP) with 10,369 (40.92%). Source: Elections BC Statement of Votes.

What was voter turnout in Kamloops Centre in 2024?

25,374 of 44,059 registered voters voted in Kamloops Centre, a turnout of 57.59%, compared with 58.45% province-wide.

How close was the race in Kamloops Centre?

The winning margin was 2,003 votes (7.91 percentage points), the 31st closest of BC's 93 ridings.
