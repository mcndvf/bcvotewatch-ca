Prince George-Valemount

Rosalyn Bird (Conservative) won Prince George-Valemount in the October 19, 2024 BC election with 55.19% of the vote, 3,309 votes ahead of Clay Pountney (NDP). Turnout was 53.60%.

How close was it?

Prince George-Valemount ranks 65 of 93 among the closest races in 2024 (1 is the closest). The winning margin was 3,309 votes (20.25 percentage points); it would have taken 1,655 voters switching from Rosalyn Bird to Clay Pountney to flip the result. Province-wide the Conservative took 43.28% of the vote, compared with 55.19% here.
