Nanaimo-Gabriola Island

Sheila Malcolmson (NDP) won Nanaimo-Gabriola Island in the October 19, 2024 BC election with 52.75% of the vote, 5,030 votes ahead of Dale Parker (Conservative). Turnout was 59.96%.

How close was it?

Nanaimo-Gabriola Island ranks 60 of 93 among the closest races in 2024 (1 is the closest). The winning margin was 5,030 votes (18.10 percentage points); it would have taken 2,516 voters switching from Sheila Malcolmson to Dale Parker to flip the result. Province-wide the NDP took 44.87% of the vote, compared with 52.75% here.

Nanaimo-Gabriola Island FAQ

Who won Nanaimo-Gabriola Island in the 2024 BC election?

Sheila Malcolmson (NDP) won Nanaimo-Gabriola Island with 14,663 votes (52.75%), ahead of Dale Parker (Conservative) with 9,633 (34.65%). Source: Elections BC Statement of Votes.

What was voter turnout in Nanaimo-Gabriola Island in 2024?

27,855 of 46,454 registered voters voted in Nanaimo-Gabriola Island, a turnout of 59.96%, compared with 58.45% province-wide.

How close was the race in Nanaimo-Gabriola Island?

The winning margin was 5,030 votes (18.10 percentage points), the 60th closest of BC's 93 ridings.
