Surrey South

Brent Chapman (Conservative) won Surrey South in the October 19, 2024 BC election with 58.83% of the vote, 3,920 votes ahead of Haroon Ghaffar (NDP). Turnout was 56.29%.

How close was it?

Surrey South ranks 58 of 93 among the closest races in 2024 (1 is the closest). The winning margin was 3,920 votes (17.66 percentage points); it would have taken 1,961 voters switching from Brent Chapman to Haroon Ghaffar to flip the result. Province-wide the Conservative took 43.28% of the vote, compared with 58.83% here.

Surrey South FAQ

Who won Surrey South in the 2024 BC election?

Brent Chapman (Conservative) won Surrey South with 13,056 votes (58.83%), ahead of Haroon Ghaffar (NDP) with 9,136 (41.17%). Source: Elections BC Statement of Votes.

What was voter turnout in Surrey South in 2024?

22,311 of 39,633 registered voters voted in Surrey South, a turnout of 56.29%, compared with 58.45% province-wide.

How close was the race in Surrey South?

The winning margin was 3,920 votes (17.66 percentage points), the 58th closest of BC's 93 ridings.
