North Island

Anna Kindy (Conservative) won North Island in the October 19, 2024 BC election with 47.07% of the vote, 639 votes ahead of Michele Babchuk (NDP). Turnout was 62.15%.

How close was it?

North Island ranks 12 of 93 among the closest races in 2024 (1 is the closest). The winning margin was 639 votes (2.14 percentage points); it would have taken 320 voters switching from Anna Kindy to Michele Babchuk to flip the result. Province-wide the Conservative took 43.28% of the vote, compared with 47.07% here.

North Island FAQ

Who won North Island in the 2024 BC election?

Anna Kindy (Conservative) won North Island with 14,100 votes (47.07%), ahead of Michele Babchuk (NDP) with 13,461 (44.93%). Source: Elections BC Statement of Votes.

What was voter turnout in North Island in 2024?

29,989 of 48,249 registered voters voted in North Island, a turnout of 62.15%, compared with 58.45% province-wide.

How close was the race in North Island?

The winning margin was 639 votes (2.14 percentage points), the 12th closest of BC's 93 ridings.
