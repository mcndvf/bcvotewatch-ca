Burnaby South-Metrotown

Paul Choi (NDP) won Burnaby South-Metrotown in the October 19, 2024 BC election with 49.33% of the vote, 1,187 votes ahead of Han Lee (Conservative). Turnout was 48.85%.

How close was it?

Burnaby South-Metrotown ranks 30 of 93 among the closest races in 2024 (1 is the closest). The winning margin was 1,187 votes (7.74 percentage points); it would have taken 594 voters switching from Paul Choi to Han Lee to flip the result. Province-wide the NDP took 44.87% of the vote, compared with 49.33% here.

Burnaby South-Metrotown FAQ

Who won Burnaby South-Metrotown in the 2024 BC election?

What was voter turnout in Burnaby South-Metrotown in 2024?

How close was the race in Burnaby South-Metrotown?
