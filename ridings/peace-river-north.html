Peace River North

Jordan Kealy (Conservative) won Peace River North in the October 19, 2024 BC election with 74.36% of the vote, 8,175 votes ahead of Dan Davies (Independent). Turnout was 57.16%.

How close was it?

Peace River North ranks 93 of 93 among the closest races in 2024 (1 is the closest). The winning margin was 8,175 votes (54.21 percentage points); it would have taken 4,088 voters switching from Jordan Kealy to Dan Davies to flip the result. Province-wide the Conservative took 43.28% of the vote, compared with 74.36% here.
