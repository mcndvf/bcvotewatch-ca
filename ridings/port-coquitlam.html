Port Coquitlam

Mike Farnworth (NDP) won Port Coquitlam in the October 19, 2024 BC election with 53.87% of the vote, 3,876 votes ahead of Keenan Adams (Conservative). Turnout was 57.70%.

How close was it?

Port Coquitlam ranks 53 of 93 among the closest races in 2024 (1 is the closest). The winning margin was 3,876 votes (15.08 percentage points); it would have taken 1,939 voters switching from Mike Farnworth to Keenan Adams to flip the result. Province-wide the NDP took 44.87% of the vote, compared with 53.87% here.

Port Coquitlam FAQ

Who won Port Coquitlam in the 2024 BC election?

Mike Farnworth (NDP) won Port Coquitlam with 13,843 votes (53.87%), ahead of Keenan Adams (Conservative) with 9,967 (38.79%). Source: Elections BC Statement of Votes.

What was voter turnout in Port Coquitlam in 2024?

25,736 of 44,601 registered voters voted in Port Coquitlam, a turnout of 57.70%, compared with 58.45% province-wide.

How close was the race in Port Coquitlam?

The winning margin was 3,876 votes (15.08 percentage points), the 53rd closest of BC's 93 ridings.
