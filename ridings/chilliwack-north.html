Chilliwack North

Heather Maahs (Conservative) won Chilliwack North in the October 19, 2024 BC election with 54.58% of the vote, 3,651 votes ahead of Dan Coulter (NDP). Turnout was 55.07%.

How close was it?

Chilliwack North ranks 55 of 93 among the closest races in 2024 (1 is the closest). The winning margin was 3,651 votes (16.92 percentage points); it would have taken 1,826 voters switching from Heather Maahs to Dan Coulter to flip the result. Province-wide the Conservative took 43.28% of the vote, compared with 54.58% here.

Chilliwack North FAQ

Who won Chilliwack North in the 2024 BC election?

Heather Maahs (Conservative) won Chilliwack North with 11,776 votes (54.58%), ahead of Dan Coulter (NDP) with 8,125 (37.66%). Source: Elections BC Statement of Votes.

What was voter turnout in Chilliwack North in 2024?

21,601 of 39,227 registered voters voted in Chilliwack North, a turnout of 55.07%, compared with 58.45% province-wide.

How close was the race in Chilliwack North?

The winning margin was 3,651 votes (16.92 percentage points), the 55th closest of BC's 93 ridings.
