Vancouver-Langara

Sunita Dhir (NDP) won Vancouver-Langara in the October 19, 2024 BC election with 48.43% of the vote, 419 votes ahead of Bryan Breguet (Conservative). Turnout was 51.83%.

How close was it?

Vancouver-Langara ranks 14 of 93 among the closest races in 2024 (1 is the closest). The winning margin was 419 votes (2.38 percentage points); it would have taken 210 voters switching from Sunita Dhir to Bryan Breguet to flip the result. Province-wide the NDP took 44.87% of the vote, compared with 48.43% here.

The seat after 2024

Vancouver-Langara FAQ

Who won Vancouver-Langara in the 2024 BC election?

Sunita Dhir (NDP) won Vancouver-Langara with 8,506 votes (48.43%), ahead of Bryan Breguet (Conservative) with 8,087 (46.05%). Source: Elections BC Statement of Votes.

What was voter turnout in Vancouver-Langara in 2024?

17,601 of 33,961 registered voters voted in Vancouver-Langara, a turnout of 51.83%, compared with 58.45% province-wide.

How close was the race in Vancouver-Langara?

The winning margin was 419 votes (2.38 percentage points), the 14th closest of BC's 93 ridings.
