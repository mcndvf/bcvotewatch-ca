Boundary-Similkameen

Donegal Wilson (Conservative) won Boundary-Similkameen in the October 19, 2024 BC election with 48.39% of the vote, 1,438 votes ahead of Roly Russell (NDP). Turnout was 62.74%.

How close was it?

Boundary-Similkameen ranks 23 of 93 among the closest races in 2024 (1 is the closest). The winning margin was 1,438 votes (5.83 percentage points); it would have taken 720 voters switching from Donegal Wilson to Roly Russell to flip the result. Province-wide the Conservative took 43.28% of the vote, compared with 48.39% here.
