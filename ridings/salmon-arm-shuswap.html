Salmon Arm-Shuswap

David L. Williams (Conservative) won Salmon Arm-Shuswap in the October 19, 2024 BC election with 52.11% of the vote, 6,889 votes ahead of Sylvia Lindgren (NDP). Turnout was 61.42%.

How close was it?

Salmon Arm-Shuswap ranks 68 of 93 among the closest races in 2024 (1 is the closest).
