West Kelowna-Peachland

Macklin McCall (Conservative) won West Kelowna-Peachland in the October 19, 2024 BC election with 50.85% of the vote, 6,081 votes ahead of Krystal Smith (NDP). Turnout was 58.36%.

How close was it?

West Kelowna-Peachland ranks 72 of 93 among the closest races in 2024 (1 is the closest). The winning margin was 6,081 votes (22.95 percentage points); it would have taken 3,041 voters switching from Macklin McCall to Krystal Smith to flip the result. Province-wide the Conservative took 43.28% of the vote, compared with 50.85% here.
