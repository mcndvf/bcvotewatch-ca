Richmond-Queensborough

Steve Kooner (Conservative) won Richmond-Queensborough in the October 19, 2024 BC election with 50.91% of the vote, 1,339 votes ahead of Aman Singh (NDP). Turnout was 53.01%.

How close was it?

Richmond-Queensborough ranks 27 of 93 among the closest races in 2024 (1 is the closest). The winning margin was 1,339 votes (6.78 percentage points); it would have taken 670 voters switching from Steve Kooner to Aman Singh to flip the result. Province-wide the Conservative took 43.28% of the vote, compared with 50.91% here.

Richmond-Queensborough FAQ

Who won Richmond-Queensborough in the 2024 BC election?

Steve Kooner (Conservative) won Richmond-Queensborough with 10,052 votes (50.91%), ahead of Aman Singh (NDP) with 8,713 (44.13%). Source: Elections BC Statement of Votes.

What was voter turnout in Richmond-Queensborough in 2024?

19,807 of 37,364 registered voters voted in Richmond-Queensborough, a turnout of 53.01%, compared with 58.45% province-wide.

How close was the race in Richmond-Queensborough?

The winning margin was 1,339 votes (6.78 percentage points), the 27th closest of BC's 93 ridings.
