Burnaby-New Westminster

Raj Chouhan (NDP) won Burnaby-New Westminster in the October 19, 2024 BC election with 59.99% of the vote, 4,486 votes ahead of Deepak Suri (Conservative). Turnout was 51.26%.

How close was it?

Burnaby-New Westminster ranks 74 of 93 among the closest races in 2024 (1 is the closest). The winning margin was 4,486 votes (25.28 percentage points); it would have taken 2,244 voters switching from Raj Chouhan to Deepak Suri to flip the result. Province-wide the NDP took 44.87% of the vote, compared with 59.99% here.

Burnaby-New Westminster FAQ

Who won Burnaby-New Westminster in the 2024 BC election?

Raj Chouhan (NDP) won Burnaby-New Westminster with 10,647 votes (59.99%), ahead of Deepak Suri (Conservative) with 6,161 (34.71%). Source: Elections BC Statement of Votes.

What was voter turnout in Burnaby-New Westminster in 2024?

17,837 of 34,794 registered voters voted in Burnaby-New Westminster, a turnout of 51.26%, compared with 58.45% province-wide.

How close was the race in Burnaby-New Westminster?

The winning margin was 4,486 votes (25.28 percentage points), the 74th closest of BC's 93 ridings.
