Chilliwack-Cultus Lake

A'aliya Warbus (Conservative) won Chilliwack-Cultus Lake in the October 19, 2024 BC election with 54.58% of the vote, 2,290 votes ahead of Kelli Paddon (NDP). Turnout was 60.37%.

How close was it?

Chilliwack-Cultus Lake ranks 34 of 93 among the closest races in 2024 (1 is the closest). The winning margin was 2,290 votes (9.16 percentage points); it would have taken 1,146 voters switching from A'aliya Warbus to Kelli Paddon to flip the result. Province-wide the Conservative took 43.28% of the vote, compared with 54.58% here.

Chilliwack-Cultus Lake FAQ

Who won Chilliwack-Cultus Lake in the 2024 BC election?

A'aliya Warbus (Conservative) won Chilliwack-Cultus Lake with 13,656 votes (54.58%), ahead of Kelli Paddon (NDP) with 11,366 (45.42%). Source: Elections BC Statement of Votes.

What was voter turnout in Chilliwack-Cultus Lake in 2024?

25,105 of 41,587 registered voters voted in Chilliwack-Cultus Lake, a turnout of 60.37%, compared with 58.45% province-wide.

How close was the race in Chilliwack-Cultus Lake?

The winning margin was 2,290 votes (9.16 percentage points), the 34th closest of BC's 93 ridings.
