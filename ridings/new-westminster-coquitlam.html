New Westminster-Coquitlam

Jennifer Whiteside (NDP) won New Westminster-Coquitlam in the October 19, 2024 BC election with 59.13% of the vote, 6,319 votes ahead of Ndellie Massey (Conservative). Turnout was 54.05%.

How close was it?

New Westminster-Coquitlam ranks 77 of 93 among the closest races in 2024 (1 is the closest). The winning margin was 6,319 votes (29.29 percentage points); it would have taken 3,160 voters switching from Jennifer Whiteside to Ndellie Massey to flip the result. Province-wide the NDP took 44.87% of the vote, compared with 59.13% here.

The seat after 2024

New Westminster-Coquitlam FAQ

Who won New Westminster-Coquitlam in the 2024 BC election?

Jennifer Whiteside (NDP) won New Westminster-Coquitlam with 12,757 votes (59.13%), ahead of Ndellie Massey (Conservative) with 6,438 (29.84%). Source: Elections BC Statement of Votes.

What was voter turnout in New Westminster-Coquitlam in 2024?

21,631 of 40,020 registered voters voted in New Westminster-Coquitlam, a turnout of 54.05%, compared with 58.45% province-wide.

How close was the race in New Westminster-Coquitlam?

The winning margin was 6,319 votes (29.29 percentage points), the 77th closest of BC's 93 ridings.
