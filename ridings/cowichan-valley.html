Cowichan Valley

Debra Toporowski (NDP) won Cowichan Valley in the October 19, 2024 BC election with 40.51% of the vote, 849 votes ahead of John Koury (Conservative). Turnout was 63.52%.

How close was it?

Cowichan Valley ranks 17 of 93 among the closest races in 2024 (1 is the closest). The winning margin was 849 votes (2.92 percentage points); it would have taken 425 voters switching from Debra Toporowski to John Koury to flip the result. Province-wide the NDP took 44.87% of the vote, compared with 40.51% here.

Cowichan Valley FAQ

Who won Cowichan Valley in the 2024 BC election?

Debra Toporowski (NDP) won Cowichan Valley with 11,795 votes (40.51%), ahead of John Koury (Conservative) with 10,946 (37.59%). Source: Elections BC Statement of Votes.

What was voter turnout in Cowichan Valley in 2024?

29,157 of 45,901 registered voters voted in Cowichan Valley, a turnout of 63.52%, compared with 58.45% province-wide.
