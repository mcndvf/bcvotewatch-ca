Kootenay Central

Brittny Anderson (NDP) won Kootenay Central in the October 19, 2024 BC election with 39.63% of the vote, 1,749 votes ahead of Kelly Vandenberghe (Conservative). Turnout was 64.28%.

How close was it?

Kootenay Central ranks 32 of 93 among the closest races in 2024 (1 is the closest). The winning margin was 1,749 votes (7.96 percentage points); it would have taken 875 voters switching from Brittny Anderson to Kelly Vandenberghe to flip the result. Province-wide the NDP took 44.87% of the vote, compared with 39.63% here.

Who won Kootenay Central in the 2024 BC election?

What was voter turnout in Kootenay Central in 2024?
