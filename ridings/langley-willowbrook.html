Langley-Willowbrook

Jody Toor (Conservative) won Langley-Willowbrook in the October 19, 2024 BC election with 48.24% of the vote, 867 votes ahead of Andrew Mercier (NDP). Turnout was 55.44%.

How close was it?

Langley-Willowbrook ranks 20 of 93 among the closest races in 2024 (1 is the closest). The winning margin was 867 votes (3.81 percentage points); it would have taken 434 voters switching from Jody Toor to Andrew Mercier to flip the result. Province-wide the Conservative took 43.28% of the vote, compared with 48.24% here.
