Vancouver-West End

Spencer Chandra Herbert (NDP) won Vancouver-West End in the October 19, 2024 BC election with 63.01% of the vote, 7,466 votes ahead of Jon Ellacott (Conservative). Turnout was 53.56%.

How close was it?

Vancouver-West End ranks 86 of 93 among the closest races in 2024 (1 is the closest). The winning margin was 7,466 votes (35.79 percentage points); it would have taken 3,734 voters switching from Spencer Chandra Herbert to Jon Ellacott to flip the result. Province-wide the NDP took 44.87% of the vote, compared with 63.01% here.

Vancouver-West End FAQ

Who won Vancouver-West End in the 2024 BC election?

Spencer Chandra Herbert (NDP) won Vancouver-West End with 13,143 votes (63.01%), ahead of Jon Ellacott (Conservative) with 5,677 (27.22%). Source: Elections BC Statement of Votes.

What was voter turnout in Vancouver-West End in 2024?

20,894 of 39,011 registered voters voted in Vancouver-West End, a turnout of 53.56%, compared with 58.45% province-wide.

How close was the race in Vancouver-West End?

The winning margin was 7,466 votes (35.79 percentage points), the 86th closest of BC's 93 ridings.
