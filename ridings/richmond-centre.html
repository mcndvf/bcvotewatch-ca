Richmond Centre

Hon Chan (Conservative) won Richmond Centre in the October 19, 2024 BC election with 51.99% of the vote, 2,465 votes ahead of Henry Yao (NDP). Turnout was 49.07%.

How close was it?

Richmond Centre ranks 54 of 93 among the closest races in 2024 (1 is the closest). The winning margin was 2,465 votes (15.21 percentage points); it would have taken 1,233 voters switching from Hon Chan to Henry Yao to flip the result. Province-wide the Conservative took 43.28% of the vote, compared with 51.99% here.

Richmond Centre FAQ

Who won Richmond Centre in the 2024 BC election?

Hon Chan (Conservative) won Richmond Centre with 8,426 votes (51.99%), ahead of Henry Yao (NDP) with 5,961 (36.78%). Source: Elections BC Statement of Votes.

What was voter turnout in Richmond Centre in 2024?
