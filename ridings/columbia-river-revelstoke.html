Columbia River-Revelstoke

Scott McInnis (Conservative) won Columbia River-Revelstoke in the October 19, 2024 BC election with 47.92% of the vote, 670 votes ahead of Andrea Dunlop (NDP). Turnout was 58.46%.

How close was it?

Columbia River-Revelstoke ranks 18 of 93 among the closest races in 2024 (1 is the closest). The winning margin was 670 votes (3.66 percentage points); it would have taken 336 voters switching from Scott McInnis to Andrea Dunlop to flip the result. Province-wide the Conservative took 43.28% of the vote, compared with 47.92% here.
