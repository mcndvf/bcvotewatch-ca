West Vancouver-Capilano

Lynne Block (Conservative) won West Vancouver-Capilano in the October 19, 2024 BC election with 46.67% of the vote, 5,043 votes ahead of Sara Eftekhar (NDP). Turnout was 58.62%.

How close was it?

West Vancouver-Capilano ranks 63 of 93 among the closest races in 2024 (1 is the closest). The winning margin was 5,043 votes (19.53 percentage points); it would have taken 2,522 voters switching from Lynne Block to Sara Eftekhar to flip the result. Province-wide the Conservative took 43.28% of the vote, compared with 46.67% here.
