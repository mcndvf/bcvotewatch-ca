Nechako Lakes

John Rustad (Conservative) won Nechako Lakes in the October 19, 2024 BC election with 67.45% of the vote, 4,684 votes ahead of Murphy Abraham (NDP). Turnout was 63.37%.

How close was it?

Nechako Lakes ranks 90 of 93 among the closest races in 2024 (1 is the closest). The winning margin was 4,684 votes (40.24 percentage points); it would have taken 2,343 voters switching from John Rustad to Murphy Abraham to flip the result. Province-wide the Conservative took 43.28% of the vote, compared with 67.45% here.

Nechako Lakes FAQ

Who won Nechako Lakes in the 2024 BC election?

John Rustad (Conservative) won Nechako Lakes with 7,851 votes (67.45%), ahead of Murphy Abraham (NDP) with 3,167 (27.21%). Source: Elections BC Statement of Votes.

What was voter turnout in Nechako Lakes in 2024?

11,665 of 18,407 registered voters voted in Nechako Lakes, a turnout of 63.37%, compared with 58.45% province-wide.

How close was the race in Nechako Lakes?

The winning margin was 4,684 votes (40.24 percentage points), the 90th closest of BC's 93 ridings.
