Vancouver-South Granville

Brenda Bailey (NDP) won Vancouver-South Granville in the October 19, 2024 BC election with 64.31% of the vote, 10,530 votes ahead of Aron Lageri (Conservative). Turnout was 63.82%.

How close was it?

Vancouver-South Granville ranks 88 of 93 among the closest races in 2024 (1 is the closest). The winning margin was 10,530 votes (39.35 percentage points); it would have taken 5,266 voters switching from Brenda Bailey to Aron Lageri to flip the result. Province-wide the NDP took 44.87% of the vote, compared with 64.31% here.
