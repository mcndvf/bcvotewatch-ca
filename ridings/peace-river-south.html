Peace River South

Larry Neufeld (Conservative) won Peace River South in the October 19, 2024 BC election with 69.84% of the vote, 4,657 votes ahead of Mike Bernier (Unaffiliated). Turnout was 56.56%.

How close was it?

Peace River South ranks 91 of 93 among the closest races in 2024 (1 is the closest). The winning margin was 4,657 votes (45.28 percentage points); it would have taken 2,329 voters switching from Larry Neufeld to Mike Bernier to flip the result. Province-wide the Conservative took 43.28% of the vote, compared with 69.84% here.

Peace River South FAQ

Who won Peace River South in the 2024 BC election?

What was voter turnout in Peace River South in 2024?

10,298 of 18,206 registered voters voted in Peace River South, a turnout of 56.56%, compared with 58.45% province-wide.
