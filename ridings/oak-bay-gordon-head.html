Oak Bay-Gordon Head

Diana Gibson (NDP) won Oak Bay-Gordon Head in the October 19, 2024 BC election with 49.10% of the vote, 5,977 votes ahead of Stephen Andrew (Conservative). Turnout was 69.80%.

How close was it?

Oak Bay-Gordon Head ranks 64 of 93 among the closest races in 2024 (1 is the closest).
