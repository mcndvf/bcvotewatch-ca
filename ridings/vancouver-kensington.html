BC provincial riding

Vancouver-Kensington

Mable Elmore (NDP) won Vancouver-Kensington in the October 19, 2024 BC election with 60.90% of the vote, 5,652 votes ahead of Syed Mohsin (Conservative). Turnout was 51.57%.

How close was it?

Vancouver-Kensington ranks 78 of 93 among the closest races in 2024 (1 is the closest). The winning margin was 5,652 votes (29.38 percentage points); it would have taken 2,827 voters switching from Mable Elmore to Syed Mohsin to flip the result. Province-wide the NDP took 44.87% of the vote, compared with 60.90% here.

The seat after 2024

These are 2024 election results. The person who holds a seat can change between general elections through resignations, by-elections or a change of party, so use the Legislative Assembly’s member directory for the current MLA.

Vancouver-Kensington FAQ

Who won Vancouver-Kensington in the 2024 BC election?

Mable Elmore (NDP) won Vancouver-Kensington with 11,713 votes (60.90%), ahead of Syed Mohsin (Conservative) with 6,061 (31.52%). Source: Elections BC Statement of Votes.

What was voter turnout in Vancouver-Kensington in 2024?

19,285 of 37,396 registered voters voted in Vancouver-Kensington, a turnout of 51.57%, compared with 58.45% province-wide.

How close was the race in Vancouver-Kensington?

The winning margin was 5,652 votes (29.38 percentage points), the 78th closest of BC's 93 ridings.
