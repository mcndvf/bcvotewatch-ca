Ladysmith-Oceanside

Stephanie Higginson (NDP) won Ladysmith-Oceanside in the October 19, 2024 BC election with 41.49% of the vote, 2,047 votes ahead of Brett Fee (Conservative). Turnout was 69.08%.

How close was it?

Ladysmith-Oceanside ranks 24 of 93 among the closest races in 2024 (1 is the closest). The winning margin was 2,047 votes (6.01 percentage points); it would have taken 1,024 voters switching from Stephanie Higginson to Brett Fee to flip the result. Province-wide the NDP took 44.87% of the vote, compared with 41.49% here.

Ladysmith-Oceanside FAQ

Who won Ladysmith-Oceanside in the 2024 BC election?

Stephanie Higginson (NDP) won Ladysmith-Oceanside with 14,144 votes (41.49%), ahead of Brett Fee (Conservative) with 12,097 (35.48%). Source: Elections BC Statement of Votes.

What was voter turnout in Ladysmith-Oceanside in 2024?

34,116 of 49,387 registered voters voted in Ladysmith-Oceanside, a turnout of 69.08%, compared with 58.45% province-wide.

How close was the race in Ladysmith-Oceanside?
