Abbotsford South

Bruce Banman (Conservative) won Abbotsford South in the October 19, 2024 BC election with 61.61% of the vote, 5,599 votes ahead of Sarah Kooner (NDP). Turnout was 51.72%.

How close was it?

Abbotsford South ranks 75 of 93 among the closest races in 2024 (1 is the closest). The winning margin was 5,599 votes (26.43 percentage points); it would have taken 2,800 voters switching from Bruce Banman to Sarah Kooner to flip the result. Province-wide the Conservative took 43.28% of the vote, compared with 61.61% here.

Abbotsford South FAQ

Who won Abbotsford South in the 2024 BC election?

Bruce Banman (Conservative) won Abbotsford South with 13,053 votes (61.61%), ahead of Sarah Kooner (NDP) with 7,454 (35.18%). Source: Elections BC Statement of Votes.

What was voter turnout in Abbotsford South in 2024?

21,242 of 41,068 registered voters voted in Abbotsford South, a turnout of 51.72%, compared with 58.45% province-wide.

How close was the race in Abbotsford South?

The winning margin was 5,599 votes (26.43 percentage points), the 75th closest of BC's 93 ridings.
