Kelowna-Mission

Gavin Dew (Conservative) won Kelowna-Mission in the October 19, 2024 BC election with 51.49% of the vote, 5,158 votes ahead of Harpreet Badohal (NDP). Turnout was 60.00%.

How close was it?

Kelowna-Mission ranks 62 of 93 among the closest races in 2024 (1 is the closest). The winning margin was 5,158 votes (18.88 percentage points); it would have taken 2,580 voters switching from Gavin Dew to Harpreet Badohal to flip the result. Province-wide the Conservative took 43.28% of the vote, compared with 51.49% here.

Kelowna-Mission FAQ

Who won Kelowna-Mission in the 2024 BC election?

Gavin Dew (Conservative) won Kelowna-Mission with 14,071 votes (51.49%), ahead of Harpreet Badohal (NDP) with 8,913 (32.61%). Source: Elections BC Statement of Votes.

What was voter turnout in Kelowna-Mission in 2024?

27,372 of 45,617 registered voters voted in Kelowna-Mission, a turnout of 60.00%, compared with 58.45% province-wide.

How close was the race in Kelowna-Mission?

The winning margin was 5,158 votes (18.88 percentage points), the 62nd closest of BC's 93 ridings.
