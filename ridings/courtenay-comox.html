Courtenay-Comox

Brennan Day (Conservative) won Courtenay-Comox in the October 19, 2024 BC election with 38.83% of the vote, 93 votes ahead of Ronna-Rae Leonard (NDP). Turnout was 68.71%.

How close was it?

Courtenay-Comox ranks 3 of 93 among the closest races in 2024 (1 is the closest). The winning margin was 93 votes (0.27 percentage points); it would have taken 47 voters switching from Brennan Day to Ronna-Rae Leonard to flip the result. Province-wide the Conservative took 43.28% of the vote, compared with 38.83% here.

Courtenay-Comox FAQ

Who won Courtenay-Comox in the 2024 BC election?

Brennan Day (Conservative) won Courtenay-Comox with 13,481 votes (38.83%), ahead of Ronna-Rae Leonard (NDP) with 13,388 (38.56%). Source: Elections BC Statement of Votes.

What was voter turnout in Courtenay-Comox in 2024?

34,755 of 50,583 registered voters voted in Courtenay-Comox, a turnout of 68.71%, compared with 58.45% province-wide.

How close was the race in Courtenay-Comox?

The winning margin was 93 votes (0.27 percentage points), the 3rd closest of BC's 93 ridings.
