Saanich South

Lana Popham (NDP) won Saanich South in the October 19, 2024 BC election with 49.76% of the vote, 5,335 votes ahead of Adam Kubel (Conservative). Turnout was 67.62%.

How close was it?

Saanich South ranks 57 of 93 among the closest races in 2024 (1 is the closest). The winning margin was 5,335 votes (17.31 percentage points); it would have taken 2,668 voters switching from Lana Popham to Adam Kubel to flip the result. Province-wide the NDP took 44.87% of the vote, compared with 49.76% here.
