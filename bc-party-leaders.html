BC party leaders 2026

Who leads each party in British Columbia heading into the October 24, 2026 election, with the source for each claim.

PartyLeaderNotes
BC NDPDavid EbyPremier. Won a one-seat majority (47 of 93) in October 2024 (results summary). Called the October 24, 2026 election on September 22 (iNFOnews).
Conservative Party of BCLorne Doerkson (interim)Named interim leader September 21, 2026 by unanimous caucus vote (Comox Valley Record); MLA for Cariboo-Chilcotin, elected as a Conservative in 2024. Kerry-Lynne Findlay, elected leader May 30, 2026, resigned September 20 after 14 MLAs left the caucus since August (CTV News). She has not filed nomination papers for the October 24 general election: Elections BC's official candidate list, generated September 22, 2026, shows no registered candidates in any riding yet, since nominations stay open until October 3 (Elections BC candidate list). The Abbotsford-Mission by-election she had been contesting was cancelled when the general election was called.
BC GreensEmily LowanNamed as Green leader in the Ipsos and Research Co. polls. Two seats won in 2024.
CentreBCPeter MilobarNamed leader on September 18, 2026, replacing Mike Bernier (CTV News). CentreBC lists eight MLAs, with Milobar as leader and MLA for Kamloops Centre (CentreBC). New party that appears in polling (4% Ipsos, 5% Research Co., 8% Leger in June).

How voters rated them before the writ

Ipsos (Sept 8–14, 2026) found Eby 41% favourable / 30% unfavourable and Findlay 17% / 46%. Research Co. (Aug 12–14) recorded approval of 49% for Eby, 38% for Lowan and 34% for Findlay. These ratings predate Findlay's resignation and Doerkson's appointment. Full numbers and methods are on the BC election polls page.
