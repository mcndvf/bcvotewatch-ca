BC Election 2026

Could British Columbia vote in 2026?

Yes, an early election is possible. It has not yet been formally called. Elections BC currently lists October 21, 2028 as the next scheduled provincial election and has published Fall 2026 scenarios for an unscheduled election.

BC election 2026 FAQ

Has the 2026 BC provincial election been called?

No. As of September 20, 2026, Elections BC still describes the next scheduled election as October 21, 2028 while publishing possible Fall 2026 early-election calendars.

When could a 2026 BC election happen?

The date depends on when an election is called. For example, Elections BC's published scenario gives October 24 as Final Voting Day if the election is called September 16–22, 2026, and October 31 if called September 23–29.

Where will candidate information come from?

BC Vote Watch will separate party or candidate announcements from official Elections BC candidate records, and will label the source and date.
